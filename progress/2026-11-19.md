## Review of the per-date facts, and a changed rule (same branch, uncommitted)

An independent subagent review found that "Arctic wins when it lists the date" was wrong for the case the fields were built for.

- Finding 1: specialty dates match Arctic by trip type id and start date. A phone-only date on a day when another departure of the trip starts would take that departure's fare, length and Book button. Production `/book?trip=westwater-canyon&month=2027-08` lists `AUG 13 – AUG 15`, 3-Day, type 56, with a Book button, which is the BIPOC trip's start date. From the page alone it is not possible to tell whether that row is the BIPOC departure itself or a separate trip.
- Finding 2: `getTripTypeFare` is per trip type, so a listed date with its own price would show the standard fare and ignore the typed one.
- Finding 3: the page and the row disagreed when a match had no fare.
- New rule: what is typed for the date always wins (`priceText`, `lengthText`, `minAge`), on the page and the row. A `minAge` of 0 counts as not set, and the field now validates `min(1)`.
- New field `phoneOnly` (boolean, "Booked by phone only"). `buildSpecialtyDateRows` never matches such a date, `buildCalloutMap` skips it (no badge in the trip's dates list or on `/book`), and the date page skips the Arctic lookup, says "This date is booked by phone. Call … to book." and links "See every date" to the section top. Projected in `allTripsQuery`, `tripBySlugQuery` and the `datedTrips` entries. `pnpm typegen` rerun.
- Tests: 293/293 (TripFacts cases rewritten for the new rule plus a blank and zero case, row cases for typed-wins and phone-only, a same-day collision case for `buildSpecialtyDateRows`, a no-badge case for `buildCalloutMap`). Typecheck, lint, prettier clean.
- Verified on local dev that nothing regressed: the Theoretical Blonde page shows Arctic's Price $1,195 and 4 Days with a Book row, and the Westwater trip page still badges that date. No entry uses the new fields, so those paths are covered by unit tests only.
- Docs: architecture.md and sanity-editor-guide.md updated for the new rule and switch.
