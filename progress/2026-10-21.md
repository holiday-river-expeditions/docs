## Specialty dates on /specialty and trip pages

Branch `feature/specialty-dates` in `website/`, uncommitted, rebased onto `f5cd249` before checks. All 11 specialty trips in Sanity are `specialtyDepartures` entries on regular river trips, and no trip uses `specialtyTypes`. So the only place a date showed was a badge in Dates & Availability, the last section on the trip page. The `/specialty` family sections showed plain river trip cards (pulled in by `references()`) with no date, band or ribbon.

- `/specialty`: each family lists its upcoming dates (`SpecialtyDateList`, rows from `buildSpecialtyDateRows()` in `lib/departures.ts`). Each row is matched to its Arctic departure by trip-type id and start date for seats and length. The fare is that departure's trip type's full fare (`getTripTypeFare()` in `lib/arctic/prices.ts`), falling back to the trip's starting price. "View Trip" links to `/trips/<slug>#departure-YYYY-MM-DD`. Cards now come only from `specialtyTypes`. The query adds `datedTrips` with `specialtyDepartures[specialtyType._ref == ^.^._id]` (checked live against the dataset).
- The first pass used `getAllUpcomingDepartures()` and matched only 2 of 11 dates. That fetch doesn't page and Arctic caps a list at 50 rows. The page now calls `getUpcomingDepartures()` once per distinct `arcticTripId` (6 calls), and all 11 match.
- Trip page: `SpecialtyDatesCallout` under the info bar, from `upcomingSpecialtyDates()` (same rules as `buildCalloutMap()`, today onward).
- `DepartureList` gives the first row on each specialty date the id `departure-YYYY-MM-DD`. `DepartureHashTarget` (client) opens the "Show all" `<details>`, sets `data-highlighted` and scrolls the row to center, on load and on hashchange. It uses a data attribute, not `:target`, because client navigation from `/specialty` doesn't update `:target`. A missing row falls back to `#dates-and-rates`.
- `parseArcticIds()` moved to `lib/departures.ts` and `prices.ts` uses it. `todayISO()` is exported from `lib/arctic`.
- Checks: typecheck, `eslint src e2e`, prettier, 218 unit tests, and Playwright a11y for `/specialty` and `/trips/cataract-canyon` pass. `pnpm lint` fails on `.worktrees/seo-basics/src/sanity/types.ts`, which is not part of the app. Browser: all 11 rows show seats and fares, the Lodore Aug 26 link opens the collapsed list and tints the row, and both pages hold at 375px with no horizontal scroll.
- Docs: `project/architecture.md` (route table, `departures.ts`, a specialty dates note) and `reference/sanity-editor-guide.md` (Specialty tab, what a date typo now does).
