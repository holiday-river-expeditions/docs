## Homepage first load (website `fix/first-load` from `c0dd1c7`, uncommitted)

- Bug: the featured trips grid sat blank on first load until a scroll. `RevealObserver` watched the whole `[data-reveal-stagger]` grid with `threshold: 0.1` and `rootMargin: 0 0 -10% 0`. The grid is 1,103px tall at 1440 wide and 2,645px at 390, and only its first ~90px peeks below the hero, so 10% of it never entered the zone. A healthy browser fires IO with "not intersecting", so the 3s dead-observer failsafe never ran. Reproduced headlessly against production at 1440x800 and 390x844: first card opacity 0 after load.
- `RevealObserver.tsx`: stagger grids are observed child by child. `threshold: 0`, `rootMargin: 0 0 -8% 0`. Targets already in the viewport at mount get `data-revealed` before `html[data-reveal='on']` is set in the same task, so SSR-visible content never computes hidden and never fades. Children of one grid that intersect in the same callback get `data-reveal-step` 0 to 3.
- `globals.css`: reveal rules moved to the child level (`[data-reveal-stagger] > [data-revealed]`). The nth-child delays became `data-reveal-step` delays, so row 2 steps 0/0.1/0.2s instead of all 0.3s.
- `lib/sanity/image.ts`: `placeholderUrl(source, w, h)`, a 32px-wide crop at the same aspect and hotspot, with `blur=10&q=40&auto=format`. The hero one is 320 bytes (AVIF).
- `Hero.tsx`: `placeholderImage` prop, a bg-cover div under the `<Image>` inside the Studio-photo wrapper (so the demo hero flags hide it too). `page.tsx` passes `placeholderUrl(homepage.heroImage, PAGE_BANNER_WIDTH, PAGE_BANNER_HEIGHT)`.
- `TripCard.tsx`: `imagePlaceholder` from `tripCardProps`, layered as a second `url()` under the photo, with a `bg-holiday-grey/15` base. Covers every grid, since all of them go through `tripCardProps`.
- `layout.tsx`: `<link rel=preconnect href=https://use.typekit.net crossorigin=anonymous>`. The kit is `font-display: auto` (all 9 faces), so nav, headline and button text were invisible until about 1.2 to 1.8s on a throttled load. The font files are CORS requests, which can't reuse the stylesheet's connection.
- Tests: `RevealObserver.test.tsx` (4). Added `placeholderUrl` to the `@/lib/sanity` mocks in `page.test.tsx` and `trip-finder/page.test.tsx`. Results: 272/272 unit, typecheck, `eslint src` and prettier all clean. Plain `pnpm lint` also walks the untracked `.worktrees/` and fails there.
- Verified on local dev (homepage makes no Arctic calls) with Playwright and CDP throttling (150ms latency, about 800KB/s). The first card's opacity stayed 1 from first paint (rAF sampler). Row 2 reveals with steps 0/1/2 on scroll. A client-side nav to /trips fades its first row in. CLS 0.005 desktop and 0.0006 mobile, unchanged (the font swap on the hero h1). The built-in browser pane was hidden, so IO never fired there and the old failsafe revealed everything. Use headless checks for this.

## Homepage review and defect fixes (same branch, uncommitted)

- Review: full-page headless captures of production at 1440x900 and 390x844 with reduced motion. axe (wcag2a/aa, 21aa, 22aa) found only `link-in-text-block` on the MapLibre attribution. Compared headings and first viewport with oars.com, westernriver.com and rowadventures.com. Two defects: the Learn cards rendered `background-image:url()` because none of the four `learnContent` items has an image, and the TripsMap legend (top-3) sat on top of the "demo-only" Style/Scale/Fly-to panel (top-120px) for every visitor.
- `queries.ts` homepageQuery: `"image": coalesce(image, *[_type == "post" && "/blog/" + slug.current == ^.link][0].mainImage)`. All four linked posts have a `mainImage`. A GROQ `//` comment parses, but typegen copies it into the generated type key, so the note went into JSDoc instead. Typegen ran clean and the result type is unchanged.
- `TripsMap.tsx`: the comparison panel is gated on the new `map-controls` flag and moved to bottom-left. The legend gets a `md:hidden` "Map key" / "Hide key" toggle (`aria-expanded`) and is always open from md up. `MARKER_ANCHOR` sets raft to bottom, bike to top and outpost to right with a `flex-row-reverse` row, offset `[28, 0]` (or 8 for a dot) so the point sits under the mark's centre. Markers render in two passes, pins then labels, from the same layout. The pin pass hides its chip with `invisible` and carries `aria-label`. The label pass hides its medallion and is `aria-hidden`, `tabIndex -1`, with `pointer-events: none` on the marker and `pointer-events-auto` on the chip. Before this, Green River HQ (drawn last) covered Desolation and San Rafael, and the White Rim and Maze medallions covered Cataract's chip completely. A paint-order overlap check (DOM order, visible elements, 2px tolerance) now reports no hidden label at 1440x900 or 390x844. With `map-controls` armed, the panel shows bottom-left, clear of the key.
- Hero, per Darius ("Taller, home only"): `Hero.tsx` exports `HERO_WIDTH/HEIGHT` 2880x1200 (1440:600), and the box uses `md:aspect-[1440/600] md:max-h-[65vh]`. At 70vh the trip-row peek fell to 26px at 1280x720. At 65vh the peek is 62 (1280x720), 90 (1440x800), 125 (1440x900) and 188 (1920x1080), against 65 and 87 before. `PageBanner` stays 1440:523, and its doc comment records the split.
- Sanity: `homepage.heroImage.hotspot` was null. Set to `{x: 0.5, y: 0.64, w: 0.9, h: 0.6}` with `ifRevisionId R4dcy50…`, then x to 0.45 (`TtJrXHlb…` to `1zKTICOC…`). image-url centres the crop on the hotspot centre and ignores its size. Production picks this up within 60s, so until the code ships it shows the lower framing at 1440:523.

The phone crop shows sky, cliff, river and camp, and the rafts clear the seal. `PageBanner` has the same phone-sizing bug and was not changed.
