## Decisions

- Booking UI is an **inline expanding row** (not modal/drawer) — keeps
  context, no focus-trap overhead, external link is the natural fallback.
- Motion is **CSS-first** (no framer-motion): server-component-heavy site;
  ~35kB saved; reduced-motion trivially honored.
- Variant chips filter client-side (not searchParams) to preserve ISR.
- Axe scans the reduced-motion presentation (mid-transition color blends
  read as false contrast failures).
