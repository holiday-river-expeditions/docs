### Map ideas

Riley would like the trip map to do more than show the river line. Things worth pinning:

- **Rapids.** The significant ones by class, the big ones we scout, and any others worth naming.
- **Campgrounds.** A photo for each. People are curious where they will sleep, and it would be a plus to show which camps a booked trip uses.
- **Itinerary by section.** Which stretch is run on which day, with mileage.

Format is open. It could be pins on the existing image, a flat 2D map, icons placed on the map, or labels drawn in code. Bike trips and combo trips may not need a map at all.
